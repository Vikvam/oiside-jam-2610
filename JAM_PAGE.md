# Jam page draft

## English

**I Won't Tell You** (*To ti neřeknu*)
*A party-line horror game. Every word you say helps her save you, and helps him find you.*

You are hiding in an old house with a murderer. You found a wired phone and called your sister. She grew up here, and she knows a secret way out.

But every phone in the house is on the same line, and he can pick up any extension and listen.

- **Talk carefully.** He knows the room names and the layout. He only knows what's *inside* the rooms he has already searched. Your sister knows childhood nicknames he can't decode.
- **Listen.** A click on the line. Breathing. A clock ticking behind *his* receiver: which room is he in?
- **Silence your room.** He hears your clock, your fan, the rain on your window, and he knows which rooms sound like that.
- **Lie.** Tell her you're in the Library. He'll go and check.
- **Find the way out.** Three tries, and the wrong ones are loud.

When it's over, read **his notebook**: everything he heard, and what he made of it.

Features:
- The murderer is a transparent deduction machine (no AI), so every death is explainable.
- Your sister can be voiced live by an LLM (Claude Haiku), or by an offline scripted voice.
- Three hand-made procedural looks: Blueprint, Phosphor CRT, Ink & Paper.
- All sound is synthesised in code.
- English and Czech.
- Large and rearranged houses for replays.

Controls:
- Type to whisper, Enter to send.
- Click a room (or use arrows/WASD after Esc) to walk. Shift sneaks.
- H hides.
- Ctrl+T taps the receiver (1 tap = yes, 2 = no). Ctrl+H hangs up or picks up.
- F2 changes the look.

## Česky

**To ti neřeknu**
*Hororová hra na společné lince. Každé slovo pomáhá jí tě zachránit — a jemu tě najít.*

Schováváš se ve starém domě s vrahem. V ruce svíráš sluchátko drátového telefonu a na druhém konci je tvoje sestra. Vyrůstala tady a zná tajnou cestu ven.

Jenže všechny telefony v domě jsou na jedné lince a on může kterýkoli zvednout a poslouchat.

- **Mluv opatrně.** Zná názvy místností i půdorys. Co je *uvnitř*, ví jen u místností, které už prohledal. Sestra zná přezdívky z dětství, kterým on nerozumí.
- **Poslouchej.** Cvaknutí na lince. Dech. Tikající hodiny za *jeho* sluchátkem — ve které je místnosti?
- **Ztiš svou místnost.** Slyší tvoje hodiny, větrák, déšť na okně a ví, které místnosti tak znějí.
- **Lži.** Řekni jí, že jsi v knihovně. Půjde se tam podívat.
- **Najdi cestu ven.** Tři pokusy, a ty špatné jsou hlasité.

Na konci si přečti **jeho zápisník**: všechno, co slyšel, a co si z toho vyvodil.

## AI use (for the jam rules)

- The game was designed by the team (DESIGN.md) and built with Claude (Anthropic) as the coding agent.
- All art and sound are procedural code, with no generated or third-party assets.
- The rescuer's lines can be generated live by Claude Haiku. The murderer is plain code, with no AI.
